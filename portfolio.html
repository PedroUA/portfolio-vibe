About this Portfolio

Purpose: This digital canvas showcases my creative engineering works across UI/UX, 3D modeling, game systems, and audio synthesis.

Behind the Scenes: Developed with pure semantic HTML5, fluid modern vanilla CSS (using clamp coordinates for uncompromised resolution support from 1366x768 up to 4K displays), and vanilla JS. It features momentum parallax displacement, staggered entrance animations, and robust query cache versioning.
